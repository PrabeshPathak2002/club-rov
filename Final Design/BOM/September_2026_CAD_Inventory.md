# September 2026 CAD inventory

Historical snapshot: M4 frame-joint revision, 30 September 2026. For the current build and purchasing decisions, use the [current ROV BOM](../../Documentation/BOM.md).

The [detailed CSV](September_2026_CAD_Inventory.csv) preserves the original per-part CAD inventory. Its status labels such as “STL supplied” describe the historical source package; those CAD/print files are not supplied by a Git clone.

## Snapshot contents

- **27 printed assembly parts:** 15 collar/frame/deck/cover/handle parts plus six receiver/support parts and six separate clamp jaws. The later integral rail-slot mounts supersede the old receiver/jaw arrangement.
- **10 fit coupons** and **6 STEP interface references:** design/test aids, not installed vehicle parts.
- **38 custom frame/mount screws, 34 nuts, 56 washers and 4 inserts:** historical hardware counts for that revision.
- **30 imported enclosure/electronics screw bodies:** CAD-body counts, not a verified purchase quantity or an extra fastener kit.
- **Reference assemblies:** enclosure, six U01 thrusters, Turnigy 4S 4000 mAh battery, four-in-one ESC, ARK FPV, ESP32-P4 and camera.
- **Unspecified accessories:** tether, power distribution, additional ESC channels, seals, trim and recovery equipment were incomplete in this snapshot.

The Zeee battery, two Basic ESCs, revised thruster mounts, electronics stack and endcap selections are tracked in the current BOM. Do not combine the snapshot totals with current quantities. CAD labels and body counts do not establish ownership, compatibility or procurement requirements.
