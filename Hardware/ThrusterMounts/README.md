# U01 slide-in rail thruster mounts

I revised the thruster mounts to make them easier to print and assemble. The seven files in [STL](STL/) contain four horizontal mounts with continuous smooth supports, two rounded vertical mounts, and a rail-fit coupon. Earlier ribbed supports and removable-jaw designs are superseded. Exports are in assembly coordinates and must be oriented in the slicer.

![Support revision](preview.png)

The integral rail receiver uses nominal 0.3 mm clearance and a 60 mm receiver length. One M4 × 50 mm cross-bolt, one M4 nut and one washer retain each thruster: six of each for six mounts. The rail model I used has an approximately 5.19 mm transverse hole; check the physical rail, bolt play and retention under reversing loads. Do not tighten the printed receiver until it crushes the rail.

Print the rail coupon first in the intended PETG settings. Check sliding fit, surface finish and play before printing the full set. Channel-end-down is a candidate orientation, but integrated bayonet feet still need local support. I still need to slice and strength-test the complete parts.

On the four horizontal mounts, I replaced the ribbed span with a 32 mm-wide, 11 mm-thick capsule-shaped support and 1.5 mm exposed upper-side fillets. Receivers have rounded exterior ends; rail contact, bayonet interface and fastener arrangement are retained. The two vertical mounts use the preceding rounded receiver geometry.

The final CAD bodies were single solids with healthy fillets; local interference checks passed. Those checks do not establish physical fit, printed strength or wet-service durability. I keep the original scripts, checks and earlier exports in my private working-history archive.
